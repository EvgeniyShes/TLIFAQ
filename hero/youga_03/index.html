SCold Hound's Fangs: campaign → Profound

Key skill: Hound's Fangs (Chromatic Shot or Splitting Lightning Bolt before it)Start + endgame Budget: ●●●Hard ▲ buff
ProsSeven stages: campaign, TM4, TM7, TM8, Profound. Physical damage is converted to cold via Prophet (Extreme Coldness), then Marksman (Gale) turns projectile speed into damage. Covers all four trait branches, legendary talent priorities for slates, budget uniques (Blind Vision, Old Grudge, Undying Faith) and sealing 4 × 50% auras via Lich

SIce Lances + Tangle: acts to Profound

Key skill: Ice Lances (Tangle)Start + endgame Budget: ●●●Hard ▲ buff
ProsIce Lances right from the acts: higher base damage and added-damage effectiveness (a staff with +100 cold gives ~229), built-in +1 Jump. The Chromatic Shot nerf and the Ice Lances shotgun nerf don't affect this build. The author expects 300–600M damage by the T7 Traveler with zero investment, Profound for a few thousand FE. The author played Gemma 2 Tangle Ice Lances for three seasons (top-1 Dragon at SS13 start, Traveler in the top 3) and says Youga 3 has more damage. The spreadsheet breaks down the mechanics in detail
ConsPre-season theorycraft — the author warns not every mechanic may hold up. Rebound (Magnificent) works worse on Ice Lances now. The spreadsheet is in Russian. Followers aren't covered
