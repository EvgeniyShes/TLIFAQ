AVigilant Breeze: campaign to Deep Space

Key skill: Merge with a Magus, Magus ultimate (Thunder or Fire Magus for acts)Start + endgame Budget: ●●●Hard
ProsFast, solid defense, steady boss damage. Proven path to Profound and Deep Space: the guide author was a top Iris on SS13 progression. Two act variants: Thunder Magus is faster, Fire Magus is steadier
ConsExpensive pets (maxed Anubis roughly doubles damage), complex Magus mechanics, damage scales worse than Rosa and Rehan; Dexterity and Strength must stay equal

ARock Magus: swap from TM5

Key skill: Summon Rock Magus, Dirty Tricks (5 ailment types on minions)Start + endgame Budget: ●●●Hard
ProsLevel with fire or lightning, then around TM5, once you can craft a cane with T4–T3 mods, swap to this build. Via Dirty Tricks your minions apply 5 ailment types: Trauma, Wilt, Numbed, Scorched and cold. Isomorphic Arms (God of Machine) hands your weapon to the minions, so craft as much flat damage on the cane as possible. Video and build code included
ConsThe author advises against playing it without the pet. Needs the Machinist tree (for +10–10 base ailment damage to minions), which is weak on its own. The written guide is short, the main content is in the video

AErosion Magus: campaign to Profound

Key skill: Summon Erosion / Fire / Frost MagusStart + endgame Budget: ●●●Medium
ProsMore automated playstyle — great on mobile (easier than Rock Magus). Stages: campaign, T7, T8, Profound; up to ~1T damage. The author took it through SS13 to an SS20 kill
ConsThe Scale of Original Sin pact spirit is a must — Strength and Dexterity must be equal. Magi lose life, so you need strong regen
