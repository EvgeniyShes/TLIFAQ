ProsLevel with fire or lightning, then around TM5, once you can craft a cane with T4–T3 mods, swap to this build. Via Dirty Tricks your minions apply 5 ailment types: Trauma, Wilt, Numbed, Scorched and cold. Isomorphic Arms (God of Machine) hands your weapon to the minions, so craft as much flat damage on the cane as possible. Video and build code included
ConsThe author advises against playing it without the pet. Needs the Machinist tree (for +10–10 base ailment damage to minions), which is weak on its own. The written guide is short, the main content is in the video
