ⓘ How this tier list is made

These are not final facts but an aggregate estimate. We collect tier lists, guides, videos, spreadsheets and Reddit discussions from many sources and combine them into a weighted average: fresh data and experienced creators count for more, SS14 patch changes are factored in separately, and heroes most creators put at the top also make S.

Pets (pact spirits), gear and budget can noticeably shift a hero or build between tiers.

What changed in SS14

Key changes from the official patch notes and season preview

▲Youga 3 "The Ultimate One": new trait. Spells on the Clockwork Core auto-cast, crits reduce cooldowns, Dictator / Conqueror / Overlord branches
▲Meteor: base damage 332–616 → 465–863
▲Electric Overload: lightning bonus 15% → 16%
▼Rehan 2 (Seething Silhouette): Berserk damage +20–44% → +10–34%, Fury's Onslaught 50–78% → 40–68%. Rehan 1 unchanged
▼Selena 2 (Dance of the Deep): Terra skill damage +0–20% → −20–0%; Silencing Severance: −30% movement speed now only for 2 s after it triggers
▼Rosa 2 (Unsullied Blade): elemental damage 5–9% → 4–8% per enemy (cap 50–90% → 40–80%), Born to Cleanse 20–48% → 10–34%
▼Sage (Licorice Note): Razor Leaf and Scattered Spore weakened, Everlasting Nectar cap 60–100% → 30–70%
▼Thorns Domain, Tidal Spring, Hand of the Abyss, Thunderstorm Zone: damage reduced
▼Whirlwind / Berserking Blade: Dexterity no longer a main attribute; Arrow Rain: projectile speed bonus capped at 200%
▼Freeze builds: freeze rings nerfed (Gemma 2 unaffected in tests)
✦Tartarus prison: 16 cells, prisoners devour each other and grow, ending in a loot riot
✦Followers: mercenaries recruited from prisoners. "Six curses" combo via the Poet (chain lightning → paralysis → curses)
✦Divine Ascent: 150 floors, skip up to 5 floors, Pactspirit reward moved from floor 35 to 50
